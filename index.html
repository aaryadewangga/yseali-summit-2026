A Regional Hub for Security, Resilience, and Practical Cooperation

YSEALI Summit 2026 is an in-person regional summit in Metro Manila, Philippines, for YSEALI alumni from across Southeast Asia.

Held from 30 November to 5 December 2026, the Summit focuses on the shared security issues shaping the region, including maritime security, cyber and information security, energy security, critical infrastructure, critical minerals, supply chains, and related economic security concerns.

Through plenary sessions, expert discussions, field exposure, action plan labs, public engagement, and post-summit support, participants will connect regional issues with the work they already do in their countries and communities.

Programme Highlights

01

Regional Security Briefings

Hear from experts on Indo-Pacific security, including maritime awareness, transnational threats, energy and infrastructure security, and supply chain reliability.

02

Security in Action Site Visit

See how security cooperation works on the ground through visits to institutions and systems connected to the Summit's focus areas.

Action Plan Labs

Work in teams to develop regional and country-level plans that respond to shared security issues and set out clear next steps.

Work with mentors, speakers, and peers from the United States and Southeast Asia to test ideas, sharpen plans, and prepare them for implementation.

06

Micro-Grant Pathway

Selected teams will receive micro-grant funding, mentorship, and follow-up support to put their action plans into practice after the Summit.

Eligibility

YSEALI Summit 2026 is open to early- and mid-career professionals who meet all of the following requirements:

A citizen and resident of one of the 11 YSEALI countries: Brunei, Cambodia, Indonesia, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar, the Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, Timor-Leste, or Vietnam.
A YSEALI alumnus or alumna of an eligible fellowship, workshop, academy, summit, competition, or international exchange programme.
At least two years of professional experience in maritime, cyber, energy, infrastructure, supply chains, international affairs, or related economic security fields.
Available for the full programme, including the virtual orientation, two webinars in November 2026, and the in-person Summit in Metro Manila from 30 November to 5 December 2026.

Selection is competitive and merit-based. Applications will be reviewed based on eligibility, relevant experience, leadership potential, communication ability, and demonstrated commitment to regional security, resilience, and cooperation.

Regional Security is Connected to Everyday Systems

YSEALI Summit 2026 focuses on security challenges that affect people, economies, institutions, and cooperation across Southeast Asia.

Maritime Security

Understanding the role of sea lanes, maritime domain awareness, safe conduct at sea, and regional cooperation in protecting stability and economic activity.

Cyber and Information Security

Exploring how digital threats, cyber-enabled crime, information risks, and public trust affect national resilience and regional security.

Energy Security and Critical Infrastructure

Examining how reliable energy systems, critical infrastructure, and infrastructure protection support stable societies and reduce vulnerabilities.

Critical Minerals and Supply Chains

Looking at how reliable supply chains, critical minerals, logistics, and economic security shape competitiveness, resilience, and regional stability.

International Affairs and Regional Cooperation

Connecting security issues to diplomacy, public understanding, cooperation with the United States, and practical action across borders.

Everything you need to know before applying

Who can apply for YSEALI Summit 2026?

YSEALI Summit 2026 is open to YSEALI alumni who are citizens and residents of one of the 11 YSEALI member countries: Brunei, Cambodia, Indonesia, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar, the Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, Timor-Leste, or Vietnam.

Applicants must have at least two years of professional experience in areas related to maritime security, cyber and information security, energy security, critical infrastructure, critical minerals and supply chains, international relations or international affairs, or related economic security issues.

Do I need to live in a YSEALI member country to apply?

Yes. Applicants must currently reside in one of the 11 YSEALI member countries listed in the eligibility criteria.

Is there an age requirement?

There is no stated age minimum or maximum. However, the Summit is designed for early to mid-career professionals with relevant experience, leadership potential, and the ability to contribute actively to regional security discussions and action planning.

What professional background is most relevant?

Competitive applicants will have experience or active involvement in one or more of the Summit's focus areas: maritime security, cyber and information security, energy security, critical infrastructure, critical minerals and supply chains, international relations or international affairs, or related economic security issues.

Applicants from government, civil society, academia, media, business, technology, law, engineering, public policy, and related fields are encouraged to apply if their work connects to the Summit themes.

Do I need to attend the full programme?

Yes. Selected participants are expected to join the full programme, including the virtual pre-departure orientation, two webinars in November 2026, and the in-person Summit in Metro Manila from 30 November to 5 December 2026.

Participants should also be available for any required travel, arrival, departure, and logistics arrangements connected to the Summit schedule.

What is the U.S.–ASEAN Futures Fair?

The U.S.–ASEAN Futures Fair is the Summit's flagship public engagement event in Metro Manila. It brings Summit participants together with university students, U.S. company representatives, diplomats, speakers, and partners through action plan showcases, thematic forums, U.S. company exhibits, and public-facing features on U.S.–ASEAN and U.S.–Philippine relations.

Students will be able to engage with action plans, interact with participants, and vote for a People's Choice recognition.

What counts as YSEALI alumni experience?

Applicants must have previously participated in at least one YSEALI programme, including the YSEALI Academic Fellowship, Professional Fellowship, Regional Workshop, Women's Leadership Academy, Seeds for the Future competition, YSEALI Summit, YSEALI Fulbright Academy, or another YSEALI international exchange programme.

When do applications open?

Applications open on 14 September 2026.

When is the application deadline?

Applications close on 2 October 2026.

When will selected participants be announced?

Selected participants are expected to be announced in early November 2026.

How will applications be reviewed?

Selection is competitive and merit-based. Applications will be reviewed based on eligibility, relevant professional experience, leadership and communication skills, demonstrated commitment to regional security and resilience, and the applicant's ability to contribute to alumni-led action after the Summit.

Each U.S. Embassy will review and approve applicant shortlists for their respective country.

Is there an application fee?

No. There is no application fee to apply for YSEALI Summit 2026.

Is the Summit funded?

Selected participants will receive support to participate in the Summit. Details on covered costs, travel arrangements, accommodation, meals, and participant responsibilities will be provided through the official application and acceptance materials.

Where and when will the Summit take place?

The in-person Summit will be held in Metro Manila, Philippines, from 30 November to 5 December 2026. The virtual orientation and two pre-summit webinars will take place in November 2026.

What travel arrangements should I prepare for?

Participants should be available for any required travel, arrival, departure, and logistics arrangements connected to the Summit schedule. Full details will be shared with selected participants through the official acceptance materials.

What happens after the Summit?

After the Summit, selected teams will continue developing and implementing their action plans with micro-grant support and mentorship. Their progress may be featured through post-summit storytelling, alumni spotlights, videos, and reporting.

What is the Micro-Grant Pathway?

Selected teams will receive micro-grant funding, mentorship, and follow-up support to put their action plans into practice after the Summit. Implementation runs from January to June 2027.
